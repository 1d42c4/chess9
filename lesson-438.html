Positional logic is the habit of reading stable clues before choosing a move. In this pass, state the best plan for each side. Your evaluation is credible only if it survives the attacker trades, distracts, or deflects that piece.

The 20-second idea

Key defender

A king position may depend on one piece that guards an entry square, blocks a file, or enables exchanges.

Why this is positional logic

A positional clue does not choose the move for you. It narrows the useful plans, tells you what deserves calculation, and gives you a way to compare the quiet positions at the end of each line.
