Plan with Restriction

Positional logic is the habit of reading stable clues before choosing a move. In this pass, turn the feature into a verb: Limit the opponent's active idea while improving your own least active piece.

The 20-second idea

Restriction

A restrictive move removes useful squares or breaks, often making every later improvement easier.

Pocket phrase

Take away the move that gives them air.

The common wrong turnRestricting harmless moves while missing a real source of counterplay.
Why this is positional logic

A positional clue does not choose the move for you. It narrows the useful plans, tells you what deserves calculation, and gives you a way to compare the quiet positions at the end of each line.

Reveal the coaching answer

Restriction reduces options first; the target often appears only after the opponent becomes passive.

One stronger self-check

Before moving, say: “My move uses restriction by ___; their best reply is ___; after that reply the position is still better/equal/worse because ___.” If you cannot fill the blanks, keep looking.
